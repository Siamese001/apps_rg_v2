## Company DNA & Operating Model
Anthropic is a product- and platform-led AI company organized around Claude, with a public-benefit-corporation identity and a stated focus on reliable, interpretable, and steerable systems.

Its commercial model is increasingly enterprise-oriented: Claude serves business teams and developers, while enterprise offerings emphasize security, compliance, and administration. The company wins through model capability and trust, but
enterprise value is realized through workflow integration, change management, and production adoption.

The central operating challenge is converting a frontier-AI product into repeatable, partner-deliverable customer outcomes while preserving a coherent technical and safety standard across a growing ecosystem.

## Company Strategy & Operating Pressure
Anthropic is building a broad Claude business across direct product use, enterprise deployments, developer access, and cloud, technology, and services partners. That creates a more complex operating model than a direct-sales AI assistant
