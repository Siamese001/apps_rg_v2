security, reliability, and responsible use.

## Partnership / Ecosystem Motion
Anthropic publicly presents a partner network spanning cloud, technology, and services partners. That is meaningful evidence of an ecosystem motion rather than a purely direct, product-led model.

The partnership opportunity is strongest where partners can contribute capabilities Anthropic does not need to build internally: customer access, industry context, systems integration, implementation capacity, and complementary technology.

The likely ecosystem flywheel is:
- Cloud partners broaden access to Claude in enterprise buying and deployment environments.
- Technology partners embed or extend Claude within adjacent workflows and products.
- Services partners convert product capability into organizational adoption and delivery programs.
- Shared deployment experience can surface reusable patterns and product feedback.

A strong partner architecture function should help prevent ecosystem growth from becoming fragmented custom work. Its value is in converting partner activity into repeatable technical and commercial motions.
