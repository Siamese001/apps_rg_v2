## Company DNA & Operating Model
Anthropic is a San Francisco-based public benefit corporation and AI safety and research company. Its flagship product is Claude, positioned as an AI assistant for problem solving, including complex analysis, coding, data work, and
knowledge tasks.

Its archetype is a hybrid: frontier AI product company with an increasingly platform- and partner-led enterprise adoption motion. It wins through the Claude product experience and model capability, while enterprise reach depends on
technical delivery through cloud, technology, and services ecosystems.

The central operating challenge is to make Claude usable in consequential customer workflows while preserving the company’s emphasis on reliability, interpretability, steerability, accuracy, security, and safety.

## Company Strategy & Operating Pressure
Anthropic’s stated mission emphasizes reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. That positioning creates a commercial advantage where customers value controlled deployment, but it also raises the bar for how solutions are designed,
explained, implemented, and supported.

Claude is marketed for hard work rather than narrow consumer utility: analyzing data, writing code, reasoning through complex challenges, and supporting problem solvers. This makes architecture quality, workflow fit, and implementation
credibility central to expansion.

The operating-model pressure is to turn a rapidly evolving core product into repeatable solution patterns. That requires clear boundaries between what Anthropic standardizes, what partners build, and what customers own in their operating
environments.

## Partnership / Ecosystem Motion
Partnership is material to Anthropic’s enterprise model. Its public partner program explicitly invites cloud, technology, and services partners to solve customer AI challenges with Anthropic.

The ecosystem signal is broader than referral or resale. The partner categories imply several interdependent motions: cloud distribution and deployment, technology integrations, services-led implementation, customer solution development,
and joint market engagement.

- Cloud partners matter where customers choose deployment paths and enterprise buying is shaped by existing cloud commitments.
- Technology partners matter where Claude must fit into established application, workflow, and data environments.
- Services partners matter where customers need use-case design, change support, implementation capacity, and operationalization.
- The critical operating need is technical consistency across partner-delivered solutions, not just partner volume.
- Partner enablement should make Claude’s product, safety, security, and use-case positioning understandable enough for customer-facing technical teams to carry accurately.
