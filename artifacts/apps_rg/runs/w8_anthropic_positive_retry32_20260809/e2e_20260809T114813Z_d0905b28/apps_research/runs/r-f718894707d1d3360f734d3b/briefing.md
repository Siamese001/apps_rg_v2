## Company DNA & Operating Model
Anthropic is a San Francisco-based public benefit corporation and AI research company. Its market identity combines frontier-model development with enterprise and developer products, framed by a strong commitment to dependable,
understandable, and controllable AI.

Claude is positioned for complex knowledge work, analytical tasks, and software development. That creates an operating model in which product access alone is insufficient: customers also need practical deployment choices, organizational
readiness, governance, and confidence in how AI will be used.

The company’s differentiation is therefore not solely model performance. It also rests on whether enterprise users can adopt AI in ways that feel secure, useful, supportable, and aligned with responsible operating practices.

The company provides a general-purpose AI capability layer rather than a finished workflow for every industry. External partners are important because they bring domain context, integration skills, customer access, and change-management
capacity needed to turn platform capabilities into applied outcomes.
