Claude is positioned for both business users and developers, which makes enterprise architecture broader than API integration. Practical deployments may involve identity, permissions, organizational data, user workflows,

Anthropic’s enterprise materials emphasize security and compliance alongside capabilities for teams building with AI. That signals an environment where solution credibility depends on operational design, not model demonstrations alone.
