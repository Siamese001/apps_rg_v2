## Partnership / Ecosystem Motion

A scalable ecosystem needs more than training. It needs repeatable technical assets, partner segmentation, opportunity qualification, joint account rules, escalation paths, and feedback loops that turn delivery learning into better
products and playbooks.

The highest-value work will likely be concentrated in strategic accounts and novel implementation patterns. The operating challenge is knowing where Anthropic specialists must engage directly and where qualified partners can lead
with established approaches.

A mature program should separate partner readiness from partner autonomy. Certification, enablement, and reference materials increase coverage, while governance and shared technical standards protect customers and Anthropic’s
reputation.
